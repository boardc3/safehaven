Questions

Straight answers before you call.

Is the statement review really free?

Yes. Send one recent processing statement and we'll total every fee into one number and walk you through it. There's no obligation to switch.

What if I'm already getting a good deal?

Then we'll tell you. If staying put is your best move, we say so. You'll still leave knowing exactly what you pay.

What do I need to send?

Your most recent monthly processing statement, as a PDF or a clear photo. If you have more than one location or processor, send one from each.

Do I have to change my equipment or POS?

Not necessarily. We work with countertop, mobile, integrated POS, online carts and invoicing. If new equipment makes sense, setup and training are handled for you.

I use Square. Can you still help?

Yes. We offer Square solutions with hands-on setup and support, including flat-rate and cash discount options.

I'm already a merchant. How do I get support?

Call (866) 808-8183, email support@safehavenpayments.com, or log in to your merchant account.
